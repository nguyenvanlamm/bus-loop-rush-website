Privacy · Effective 12 October 2026

Your progress stays on your phone.

Bus Loop Rush does not ask who you are. There is no account, no advertising and no analytics. This page explains what the app keeps on your device and how purchases work.

01 / On your device

Stored by the app

The app saves only what it needs to remember your game, using your phone's own app storage (SharedPreferences on Android, UserDefaults on iOS):

  • the level you reached and the stars you earned on each level;
  • your coins and boosters;
  • your settings: sound, music, vibration and hints;
  • your daily-reward streak and the date of your last claim;
  • which tips and rule lessons you have already seen.

This data is not sent to us. It stays on the device and is removed when you delete the app or its data, unless your phone's own backup restores it.

02 / Coin packs

Purchases

Optional coin packs are sold through Google Play and the Apple App Store, under their own terms and privacy policies. The store handles your payment; the app only receives a confirmation of which pack was bought so it can add the coins, and it never sees your payment details.

To show prices and complete purchases, the app connects to the store on your device. It makes no other network requests.

03 / What we do not do

Not collected

  • No account, sign-in or player profile.
  • No advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No access to your location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share personal data, because the app does not collect any.

The game does not ask anyone, including children, for personal information.

04 / This website

The website and support forms

This site is hosted on Netlify. Netlify may log technical data such as IP addresses to run and secure the service; see the Netlify Privacy Policy. The site itself sets no cookies, runs no analytics and loads its fonts from this site, not from a third party.

The support forms are public GitHub issues and follow GitHub's terms. If you email us, we use your message only to answer you.

05 / Changes and contact

Questions
