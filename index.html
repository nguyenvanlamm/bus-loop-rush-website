Bus Loop Rush!

A crowd in eight colors loops around the island. Tap a car in the jammed lot to send it to the bus stop, and every passenger of its color hops on. Fill the bus, clear the lot, and keep the stop from jamming.

How to play

One tap per move. The trick is which car, and when.

The crowd keeps circling while you play. Send the colors that are waiting near the exit, and keep a free slot for the bus you will need next.

  1. 1

    Tap a car

    It drives straight along the arrow on its roof. If something is in the way, it bumps and rolls back.

  2. 2

    It parks at the stop

    The car loops up to the stop and takes the leftmost free slot. Four slots are open, three on HARD levels.

  3. 3

    Matching riders hop on

    Passengers of the same color leave the loop and pile onto the roof until every seat is taken.

  4. 4

    Clear the lot

    Full buses drive off. Send every car to win, but if every slot is taken and nobody can board, you are out of space.

New rules

A new twist every few levels

From level 12, new rules arrive one at a time, each with a short lesson the first time you meet it. Later levels mix up to three of them.

From level 12

Mystery cars

A "?" car shows its color once its road is clear.

From level 18

Traffic cones

Cones never move. Cars facing one are stuck behind it.

From level 24

Chained cars

A chained car breaks free after the number of cars shown has left the lot.

From level 30

VIP passengers

VIPs with a crown ride only in a big bus of their color while one is around.

From level 36

Bomb cars

Every car that leaves makes the bomb tick down. Send it before it hits 0!

From level 42

Garages

Cars wait inside. When the car in front of the door leaves, the next one rolls out.

From level 48

Flip cars

This car turns around every time another car leaves the lot. Time your tap!

From level 54

Frozen cars

An iced car thaws once a vehicle of its color fills up with passengers.

Fair, fast, and never the same twice

Short sessions, big moments: buses fill up, combos chain, and coins fly into the counter.

Every level is winnable

Levels are generated from their number, and a solver plays each one through before you see it.

HARD every fifth level

From level 10, every fifth level is a HARD level that opens with three parking slots instead of four.

Three boosters

Fill Up a bus at the stop, Shuffle the lot's colors, or lift any car out with VIP (from level 5).

Combos and stars

Fill buses within three seconds of each other for a combo, and earn up to three stars per level.

No ads

Coins come from wins and a 7-day daily reward. Optional coin packs are sold in the apps.

Plays offline

No account and no sign-in. Your progress stays on your device.

FAQ

Questions at the stop

What is Bus Loop Rush?+

A bus-and-crowd color puzzle. A crowd in eight colors loops around an island; you tap cars in a jammed parking lot to send them to the bus stop, where passengers of the same color climb aboard. Clear the lot to win the level.

How many levels are there?+

There is no fixed end. Levels are generated from their number, and every level is played through by a solver before you see it. New rules arrive at levels 12, 18, 24, 30, 36, 42, 48 and 54, and later levels mix up to three of them.

What happens when the stop fills up?+

If every open slot is taken and nobody can board, you are out of space: open one more slot for 200 coins, retry, or go home. A bomb that reaches zero, or a lot where nothing can move, also ends the run, each with its own way to keep going.

Are there ads or in-app purchases?+

There are no ads. Coins come from winning levels and from a 7-day daily reward. Optional coin packs can be bought in the Android and iOS apps.
