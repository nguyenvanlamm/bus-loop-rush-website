The game

  • A crowd in eight colors loops around an island; cars leave a packed lot, park at a stop with four open slots and fill up with passengers of their color.
  • Cars, vans and buses with 16, 24 and 32 seats.
  • Endless levels generated from their number, each played through by a solver before it is shown. From level 10, every fifth level is HARD and opens with three slots.
  • Eight rules arriving between levels 12 and 54: mystery cars, traffic cones, chained cars, VIP passengers, bomb cars, garages, flip cars and frozen cars.
  • Boosters (Fill Up, Shuffle, VIP), combos, up to three stars per level, a 7-day daily reward and a coin shop.

Interface

  • "Jewel Transit" look: glossy buttons, popups printed on bus tickets, and a win screen where coins fly into the counter.
  • A home screen built on the game's own loop road, with your next levels as bus stops.

Store

  • Optional coin packs as in-app purchases on Android and iOS. No ads.
