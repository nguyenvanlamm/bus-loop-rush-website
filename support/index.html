02 / Purchases and private questions

Email us

For a purchase problem or anything you would rather not post publicly, email lamnv260398@gmail.com. Include the date, the coin pack and your device, but never your card or account details.

Payments and refunds are handled by Google Play and the Apple App Store; we cannot see or refund payments ourselves. Ask the store for a refund, and tell us if coins did not arrive.

03 / Useful details

What helps us fix it

  • Device and system. For example "Pixel 7, Android 15" or "iPhone 13, iOS 18".
  • App version. It is shown on the store page of the app.
  • Level number. Levels are generated from their number, so the number lets us replay the exact level.
  • Steps. What you tapped, what happened, and what you expected instead.

04 / Quick answers

Before you file

My coins did not arrive after a purchase.+

Close and reopen the app with an internet connection: a purchase that was not finished is delivered again when the app starts. If the coins still do not show up, email us with the date and the pack.

Will I lose my progress if I reinstall?+

Progress is saved on your device; the game has no account or cloud save of its own. Your phone's own backup may restore it, but uninstalling usually removes it.

Can I play on several devices?+

Not with shared progress. Each device keeps its own level, stars and coins.
